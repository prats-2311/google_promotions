## 🎯 IBM Track Requirements (Hackathon-Specific)

For projects submitted under the **IBM track** in Agentic Cinema, there are explicit technical and usage requirements:

- Your project **must be built using IBM Bob** as part of the development process.  
- Use of **Confluent** is **optional but strongly encouraged** to power **real-time data** and **event-driven workflows** that make your solution more dynamic and enterprise-ready.  
- Projects that **do not demonstrate usage of IBM Bob** will **not meet the requirements** for the IBM track, regardless of how the rest of the code was written.  

To be considered a **strong submission** in the IBM track, projects should show:

- How **AI meaningfully improves a workflow**, reduces friction, or automates complex tasks.  
- How AI helps **enhance decision-making**, provide better insights, or support high-stakes operational decisions.  
- How the solution **elevates customer experience** (e.g., personalization, responsiveness, clarity) in a media or entertainment context.  
- How it drives **measurable operational outcomes**, such as reduced latency, lower costs, improved reliability, or better resource utilization.
